# Keyword Research

## Process

1. **Brainstorm seed keywords** from the user's topic/product
2. **Expand** by web_searching each seed keyword and reviewing:
   - Google Autosuggest (typed into the search box)
   - "People Also Ask" section
   - Related searches at the bottom of results
3. **Analyze search intent** for each keyword by looking at what type of content currently ranks at the top:
   - Full blog posts/guides → Informational
   - Product/category pages → Transactional
   - Comparison/review pages → Commercial investigation
   - Specific brand homepage → Navigational
4. **Assess competitive difficulty** (not 100% precise without a tool like Ahrefs/SEMrush, but can be estimated by checking):
   - Whether the top-ranking pages are large brands/high-authority domains
   - What gaps exist in the current top content (missing information, outdated, low quality) → opportunities to outrank them

## Keyword type priorities

- **Long-tail keywords** (longer, specific phrases): lower competition, higher conversion rate, suitable for new/low-authority sites
- **Topic cluster keywords**: 1 pillar page + multiple internally linked cluster articles, helping Google understand the site has deep expertise on the topic

## Notes

- Don't choose keywords just because of high volume — prioritize keywords that match the actual intent of the product/service
- For new/low-authority sites, start with long-tail, low-competition keywords, then gradually expand to broader terms
- Check for timeliness: for time-sensitive topics (pricing, products, events), use web_search to get current information rather than relying on older knowledge
