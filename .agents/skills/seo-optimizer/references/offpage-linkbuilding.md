# Off-page SEO & Link Building

## Principles
Backlinks remain one of the most important ranking factors, but **quality > quantity**. Many backlinks from spammy/low-quality sites can get a site penalized by Google (Penguin and subsequent algorithm updates).

## Ways to build sustainable backlinks

- **Linkable assets**: original research, exclusive statistics, free tools, in-depth guides — content that others naturally want to cite
- **Digital PR**: getting featured in press/industry sites through a story/data with real news value
- **Selective guest posting**: writing for reputable sites in the same industry, not sites that exist solely to sell links
- **Broken link building**: finding broken links on other sites and suggesting your content as a replacement
- **Partnerships/vendor relationships**: naturally exchanging backlinks with genuine business partners
- **HARO / expert connections**: answering journalist queries to get cited with a link

## Absolutely avoid (black-hat — can get penalized by Google)
- Mass buying/selling links / uncontrolled PBNs (Private Blog Networks)
- Link farms, comment spam, forum spam
- Mass, irrelevant link exchanges
- Unnaturally repeated keyword-stuffed anchor text at scale

If a user requests techniques from this list, explain the risks (manual or algorithmic Google penalties, long-term site-wide damage) and suggest sustainable alternatives instead.

## Other off-page signals
- **Brand mentions** without a link still have value (Google may treat this as a trust signal)
- **Social signals**: not a direct ranking factor but help increase the chance of being discovered/linked naturally
- **Google Business Profile** (for local SEO): optimize the profile, reviews, and keep NAP (Name-Address-Phone) consistent across platforms
